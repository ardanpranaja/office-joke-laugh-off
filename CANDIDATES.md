# Candidate lines

Shortlist of alternate one-liners considered for the One-Line Office Joke
Laugh-Off. The chosen line won because its punchline lands on one read, the
setup is a universal office experience, and the final twist ("in management")
turns a machine into a suspect.

1. "I told HR my workload was crushing me, so they sent me a stress ball and added 'resilience' to my job description."
   — Funny, but the punchline needs a beat too many; the joke runs long for a one-liner.
2. "Our team-building exercise was trust falls — now I trust no one, especially Dave from accounting."
   — Lands well, but naming a specific coworker narrows the joke; the printer line hits everyone.
3. "I asked for a raise and got a pizza party; the pizza was cold, the raise was colder."
   — A solid twist, but the pizza-party trope is well-worn; the judge rewards freshness.
4. "My boss said to think outside the box, so I moved my desk to the parking lot — turns out the Wi-Fi doesn't think outside the box either."
   — Clever, but the setup is long and the punchline explains itself; too wordy to land instantly.
5. "The meeting could have been an email, but the email would have needed a meeting to explain it."
   — True and relatable, yet it reads more as an observation than a joke with a sharp punchline.

## Chosen entry

> The printer only jams when the deadline is near; I think it's in management.

76 characters. One line, one setup, one twist — and every office worker has
lived the setup.
